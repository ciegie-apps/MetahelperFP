# MetahelperFP

## What it is

MetahelperFP is a tool for plex users who curate their music playlists with passion. “FP“ stands for „for plex“.  
Plex lacks some functionalities to organize music playlists. MetahelperFP fills the gaps:

- Sort playlists: Sort the playlist view in plex web and clients like plexamp using a variety of criteria and presets, create groups of playlists or drag and drop your playlists to create your custom order. 
- Merge: Merge two or more playlists and delete any resulting duplicates. 
- Search: Search a track across your playlists. Metahelper shows you the containing playlists. 
- Editor: Open a playlist and drag and drop its items to order .
- Deduplicate: Find duplicates in a playlist and remove them.



## Availability 

Metahelper will be available on ios devices soon.
